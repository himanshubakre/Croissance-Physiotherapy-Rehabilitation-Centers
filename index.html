About the clinic

Rehabilitation That Puts Your Goals First

Physiotherapy helps people move, recover and function well after injury, illness or surgery, and supports everyday movement for people living with pain or stiffness.

Croissance Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centers is a physiotherapy and rehabilitation practice in Baner, Pune. Every person arrives with a different body, history and goal, so care starts with understanding what you want to get back to and builds the plan from there. Recovery varies from person to person, and your physiotherapist will guide you on what is realistic for you.

Physiotherapy services

Care for how you move

Six areas of physiotherapy and rehabilitation. Service details are subject to confirmation by the clinic.

Orthopaedic & Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Support for movement difficulties and musculoskeletal conditions.

Spine, Neck & Back Rehabilitation

Individualized physiotherapy approaches for relevant spine and musculoskeletal concerns.

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Guided rehabilitation and movement recovery following sports-related injuries.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation focused on mobility, coordination, balance, and functional movement where appropriate.

Post-operative Rehabilitation

Rehabilitation support following surgery, according to the treating clinician's recommendations.

Women's Health & Other Rehabilitation Services

Further programmes, such as women's health, geriatric and cardiorespiratory rehabilitation, as confirmed by the clinic.
